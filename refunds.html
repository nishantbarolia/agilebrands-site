Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Our courses are digital and you get access straight after payment, so we don’t offer refunds or cancellations once a purchase is complete. Please watch the free trailer and our YouTube episodes before you buy.

When we will refund

  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • Your payment was debited but the order failed and you didn’t get access.
  • We can’t give you access because of a problem on our side that we can’t fix within 7 working days.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.

How to ask

Email learning@agilebrands.in with your order ID within 7 days of payment.
