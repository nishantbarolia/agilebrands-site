Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what we collect on agilebrands.in, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Waitlist: your name, email and the course you’re interested in, when you join a waitlist.
  • Purchases (once enrolment opens): your name, email and phone number, and the order details. Payments are handled by our payment partner; we never receive your full card or bank details.
  • Course use: lesson progress and quiz answers, so the course remembers where you are.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email.

How we use it

  • To tell you when a course you asked about opens, and to send course updates.
  • To give you access to what you bought and to support you.
  • To protect courses from shared or misused logins.

We don’t sell your information. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time.

Who processes it

  • Waitlist forms are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • Fonts are served by Google Fonts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.
  • Payments, once live, are processed by our payment gateway partner.

Your choices

Write to learning@agilebrands.in to see, correct or delete your information, or to raise a concern. We reply within 7 working days.

Cookies

This site doesn’t set advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools later, we’ll update this policy first.
