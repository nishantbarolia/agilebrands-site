Brand & marketing managers

You own the launch calendar, the brand story and the numbers behind them. These lessons give you the decision tools that sit behind the job, not the textbook version of it.

  • Run gate reviews that end in a clear call
  • Read demand before you brief a new product
  • Plan listings and campaigns for the digital shelf

Innovation & R&D teams

Overloaded with requests, many of which will never reach the market. Learn how the business side decides, so the right projects reach your bench and the wrong ones stop early.

  • Filter briefs early with clear gate criteria
  • Speak the language of the business case
  • Spend lab time on projects with a real path to shelf

D2C founders & entrepreneurs

Every launch bet is your own money, so every call has to count. Get the processes large FMCG companies use, sized for a team of a few people.

  • Launch with a decision process, not a hunch
  • Pick the listings and campaigns that convert
  • Learn from real brand cases, decision by decision
